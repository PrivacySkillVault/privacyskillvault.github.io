# Cyber Fraud

Understand how digital fraud schemes work, what evidence they can leave behind, and how investigators can separate suspicious signals from confirmed compromise.

This section focuses on account takeover, payment fraud, social engineering, digital identity abuse, authentication weaknesses, and fraud operations. Examples are educational and use fictional data unless a source explicitly documents a real event.

## Investigation mindset

A suspicious transaction is an event to investigate, not proof of a particular attack technique. Build a timeline across identity, authentication, device, session, beneficiary, transaction, and customer-contact events. Record what each source proves—and what it cannot prove.
