# Cyber Journal

**Cybersecurity, explained through evidence, investigation, and practical learning.**

The Privacy Skill Vault Cyber Journal is a learning-first publication about the security issues that matter, what the technical details actually mean, and how defenders can investigate and respond.

This is not a copied-news feed. News headlines may help identify a topic, but articles here are written after reviewing the underlying advisories, vendor research, technical documentation, and other primary evidence. Each article distinguishes confirmed facts from interpretation and illustrative scenarios.

## How an article is prepared

1. **Discover** — identify a topic from security advisories, trusted reporting, research, or community discussion.
2. **Verify** — locate primary sources and confirm dates, affected products, technical claims, and current status.
3. **Understand** — read the original material and resolve unfamiliar technical details.
4. **Explain** — write an original, plain-language explanation with the technical context and defensive implications.
5. **Review** — check factual claims, links, safety, privacy, and whether facts are clearly separated from analysis.
6. **Publish** — include references, publication date, update notes, and any limitations.

## Editorial principles

- Original explanations, not rewritten copies of other publishers' articles.
- Primary sources wherever possible; secondary reporting is attributed and cross-checked.
- Clear labels for confirmed facts, analysis, and hypothetical examples.
- No confidential employer, customer, banking, or production-system information.
- No claims that a lab test was performed unless it was actually performed.
- Corrections are made transparently when new evidence changes an explanation.

See the [editorial and source standards](editorial-standards/) before relying on an article.
