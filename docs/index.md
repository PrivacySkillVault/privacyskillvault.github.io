## How information is labelled

Accuracy matters more than presentation. Content is identified by its evidence level:

- **Professional experience** — generalised and sanitised descriptions; no employer, customer, or internal system details.
- **Lab-tested** — performed in a controlled environment, with relevant setup and limitations recorded.
- **Simulated scenario** — constructed for learning; not represented as a real incident.
- **Research note** — based on documentation or external references and not yet independently validated.

## Current areas of focus

Cyber-fraud detection and investigation, SIEM monitoring, endpoint and network telemetry, detection engineering, Linux and Windows operations, and controlled security testing.
