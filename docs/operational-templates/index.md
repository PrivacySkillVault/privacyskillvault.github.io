# Operational Templates

These templates are designed for controlled use in an organisation's approved systems. They are blank structures, not completed incident records or compliance evidence.

- [Detection Change & Validation Record](detection-change-record.md) — author, test, review and release a SIEM analytic.
- [Fraud Investigation Case Record](fraud-case-record.md) — record transaction, authentication, session and evidence links.
- [Cybersecurity Incident Report](incident-report.md) — document incident facts, response decisions, recovery and lessons learned.

**Privacy:** never publish customer details, account numbers, card data, credentials, OTPs, session tokens, production IPs, internal hostnames or employer-confidential records in PSV. Use approved internal case-management and evidence repositories.
